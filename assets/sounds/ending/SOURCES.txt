footstep_left.wav and footstep_right.wav: short mono edits of ../walker.mp3,
with softened high frequencies, shorter tails, and balanced peak levels.

shoulder_pat.wav and title_impact.wav: synthesized placeholder effects created
for this ending. No external recordings are used in these two sounds.

The WAV files contain the finished sounds; the game only plays them.
Edit volume on Footsteps and ShoulderPat in good_ending.tscn.
FootstepTiming's approach animation holds the individual step cues.

The title now uses the user's ../tsitsor.ogg, played in full by EndingMusic.
title_impact.wav is the earlier placeholder and is no longer used by the scene.
